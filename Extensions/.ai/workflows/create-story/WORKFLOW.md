# Create Story Workflow (`/evya createstory`)

## Purpose
Convert business inputs into a high-quality story draft ready for user review and eventual board creation.

## Stages
1. Intake
2. Context loading
3. Repo scan
4. Impact analysis
5. Prerequisite analysis
6. Story drafting
7. Self-review
8. Human confirmation
9. Board sync
10. Team notification
11. Artifact save

## Rules
- business-first
- no technical implementation in the business story
- ask only critical missing questions
- do not push without confirmation
- maintain high quality over speed
