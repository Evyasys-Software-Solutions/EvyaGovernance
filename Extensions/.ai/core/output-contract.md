# Output Contract

Every workflow must produce:
- a deterministic primary artifact
- a clear status
- a next action
- a human confirmation step when required

For `/evya createstory`, the primary artifact is:
- a board-ready business story draft

Minimum output sections:
- title
- epic suggestion
- business context
- problem statement
- objective
- roles
- preconditions
- workflow
- business scenarios
- acceptance criteria
- dependencies
- breaking changes
- out of scope
- assumptions
- open questions
- impacted areas
- confirmation request
