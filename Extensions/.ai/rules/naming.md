# Naming Rules

Story IDs:
- EVYA-1001
- EVYA-1002

Epic IDs:
- EP-1001
- EP-1002

Story titles:
- business outcome oriented
- easy to read in a board
- avoid implementation terms unless the business explicitly requests them

Good examples:
- Allow manual review before final submission
- Enable customer to save draft application
- Show approval status to requester
