# Quality Gates

A story can be shown to the user only if:
- the business purpose is clear
- the workflow is understandable
- roles are identified
- prerequisites are complete
- acceptance criteria are testable
- dependencies are explicit
- out-of-scope is explicit
- assumptions are visible
- open questions are listed
- repo impact has been reviewed
- technical implementation details are excluded

A story can be pushed only if:
- user confirms
- the draft passed self-review
- the board mapping is known
- epic linkage is resolved or intentionally skipped
